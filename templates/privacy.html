Privacy & the free plan.

What is stored

Your username, hashed password, hashed recovery code, session records, purchase details and receipt files. Receipts and purchases are restricted to your signed-in account. No advertising trackers or third-party analytics are included.

Where it lives

The external release runs on Render with persistent PostgreSQL on Neon. HTTPS protects browser traffic. Providers and authorised service operators can technically access stored data; this is not an end-to-end encrypted vault.

Be selective about receipts

Upload only the information needed for a return or warranty. Redact card details, home addresses and other sensitive information. Image metadata is removed. PDFs are downloaded as attachments, not displayed inline; files are not malware-scanned.

Reminders

The dashboard highlights deadlines. Calendar downloads include one-day-before alarms. You must import the file and enable alerts in your calendar. Changes require re-importing; avoid duplicate imports. No email, SMS or background push reminders are sent.

Storage & availability

The free plan supports 100 accounts, 100 purchases per account, 10 receipt files per account, and 100 receipt files across the service. Each upload is limited to 2 MB. Free hosting may sleep after inactivity, delaying startup by roughly a minute. This service has no uptime guarantee.

Export & deletion

Export purchases as CSV and download receipts individually. Trash is reversible. Permanent deletion from trash requires your password and typed confirmation; it also deletes the attached receipt. A full ZIP backup includes purchases and receipts. Account deletion removes active records and receipts; provider backups may retain older copies temporarily. Keep an independent backup of important originals.

Responsibility

Enter dates from the retailer's actual terms. A saved deadline does not establish legal return or warranty eligibility. This is an AI-assisted student project with a defined scope, not a retailer or financial service.
